Biography

Welcome to my homepage! I am currently an Assistant Professor at the College of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, Southwest Jiaotong University, China, working in the research group led by Professor Tianrui Li (李天瑞) and Professor Xun Gong (龚勋). I received my PhD from the College of Computer Science, Sichuan University, under the supervision of Professor Dezhong Peng (彭德中) [二级教授，教育部 “新世纪优秀人才”，国家自然科学二等奖 “神经网络的若干关键基础理论研究” 获得者] and Professor Peng Hu (胡鹏) [国家级青年人才].

My research primarily revolves around machine learning and optimization. I am particularly interested in topics such as multimodal learning, domain generalization, foundation models, and other emerging fields in AI and machine learning. Through my work, I aim to improve the scalability, effectiveness, and robustness of models and systems, ensuring their adaptability across diverse environments and tasks. In addition to my academic pursuits, I am actively involved in cutting-edge engineering applications aimed at bridging the gap between theoretical advancements and practical applications.

I am looking for self-motivated Undergraduate Students. If you are interested, please feel free to contact me via email.

Research Interests

  • Multimodal Machine Learning: Cross-Modal Retrieval, Federated Multimodal Learning, Partial/Noisy-label Multimodal Learning, Trustworthy Multimodal Computing
  • Visual Perception & Multimedia Intelligence: Visual Representation Learning, Person Re-Identification, High-Dynamic-Range Visual Analysis
